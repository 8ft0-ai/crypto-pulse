### What would confirm the trend

Confirmation would require BTC to continue holding above US$80,000, SOL and XRP to keep outperforming across additional hourly reports, BTC dominance to stabilise or decline, and ETH to begin participating more strongly. The interpretation would weaken if BTC loses US$80,000 support, SOL or XRP reverses sharply, ETH continues to lag while breadth narrows, BTC dominance rises further, or adverse regulatory, ETF-flow or macro headlines trigger broad de-risking.
