## Rolling trend analysis

### Emerging trend

The strongest visible pattern is a BTC-led recovery with improving large-cap breadth, while ETH participates but does not yet lead.

### Evidence from recent reports

No recent archived baseline was accessible, so rolling trend analysis is limited to current market data.

Current data shows BTC dominance at 58.3%, ETH dominance at 10.1%, BTC up 4.1% over seven days, ETH up 1.7% over seven days, SOL up 15.0%, ADA up 12.7%, LINK up 17.2%, SUI up 44.2%, ZEC up 42.8% and TON up 82.7%. This indicates that some altcoins are outperforming, but BTC remains dominant by market share. citeturn465181view0

### What is changing

Market breadth looks healthier than a narrow BTC-only move because several large and mid-large assets are positive over 24 hours and seven days. ETH’s underperformance versus BTC over seven days limits the case for a broad ETH-led risk-on phase.

### What would confirm the trend

Confirmation would require BTC to hold above the US$81,000–US$82,000 area, ETH to start outperforming BTC on the one-hour and 24-hour windows, and altcoin breadth to remain positive without a sharp liquidation spike.

### What would invalidate the trend

A BTC rejection back below the US$80,000 area, renewed ETF outflows combined with falling volume, or a broad reversal in SOL, ADA, XRP and DOGE would weaken the constructive interpretation.

### Analyst read

Fact: Current prices and breadth are positive across many major assets. Interpretation: The market appears cautiously risk-on, but still BTC-led rather than a broad speculative expansion. The delayed ETF-flow picture and elevated geopolitical uncertainty argue against over-reading the latest one-hour improvement.
