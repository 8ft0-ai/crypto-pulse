## Rolling trend analysis

### Emerging trend

The strongest emerging pattern is BTC-led consolidation above US$80,000 with selective altcoin rotation, especially towards XRP, SOL, BNB and, in the current data, ZEC and ADA.

### Evidence from recent reports

Recent CryptoPulse reports showed BTC holding around US$80,000–US$81,000, ETH near US$2,313–US$2,346, SOL repeatedly leading large-cap performance, XRP supported by regulatory optimism, and market capitalisation remaining broadly stable near US$2.75tn. The current report continues that pattern, with BTC above US$81,000, SOL up 13.35% over seven days, XRP up 2.80% over 24h, BNB up 5.28% over seven days, and global market capitalisation near US$2.71tn. citeturn806146view0turn345847news34

### What is changing

BTC dominance is higher in the current CoinMarketCap snapshot at 60.10%, compared with the roughly 58.2%–58.3% level used in recent report baselines. That weakens the argument for broad altcoin expansion, even though SOL, XRP, ADA and ZEC show strong relative pockets. ETH remains the main large-cap laggard: it is positive over 24h but nearly flat over both 1h and 7d.

### What would confirm the trend

The selective-rotation trend would be confirmed if BTC continues to hold US$80,000–US$81,000, SOL and XRP keep outperforming over multiple reports, market capitalisation holds above US$2.7tn, and BTC dominance stops rising or begins to ease without a broad sell-off.

### What would invalidate the trend

The trend would weaken if BTC loses US$80,000, SOL gives back its 7d outperformance, XRP fails to hold its regulatory-news premium, ETH remains flat while BTC dominance keeps rising, or liquidation data later show that the move was primarily leverage-driven.

### Analyst read

Fact: BTC is holding above US$81,000, global crypto market capitalisation is near US$2.71tn, and the strongest large-cap relative performance is concentrated in XRP, SOL, ADA, ZEC and BNB. Interpretation: the market is not in a broad altcoin rally; it is in a selective rotation regime where regulatory optimism and asset-specific strength are offset by rising BTC dominance and weak ETH leadership.
