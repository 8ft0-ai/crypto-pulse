## Rolling trend analysis

### Emerging trend

The dominant emerging trend remains BTC-led consolidation combined with selective rotation into higher-beta large-cap altcoins such as SOL, XRP, ADA and BNB.

### Evidence from recent reports

Recent CryptoPulse reports consistently identified BTC stabilising above US$80,000, SOL repeatedly leading 24h and 7d performance rankings, XRP outperforming during regulatory-clarity discussions, ETH lagging stronger altcoins, market capitalisation stabilising near US$2.7tn, and sentiment improving from neutral towards mildly risk-on.

### What is changing

Market breadth has improved slightly, with ADA and DOGE participating more consistently alongside SOL and XRP. However, BTC dominance remains elevated near 60%, limiting evidence of a broad altcoin regime. ETH still lacks convincing leadership characteristics.

### What would confirm the trend

The trend would strengthen if BTC remains above US$80k, SOL and XRP continue outperforming over multiple reports, BTC dominance stabilises or declines modestly, ETH begins outperforming BTC on shorter timeframes, and market capitalisation remains stable or rises further.

### What would invalidate the trend

The interpretation would weaken if BTC loses US$80k support, SOL momentum reverses sharply, XRP loses relative strength, BTC dominance rises materially further, or macro/regulatory headlines trigger market-wide de-risking.

### Analyst read

Fact: BTC remains stable while SOL, XRP and ADA continue outperforming across several reporting windows. Interpretation: the market remains selectively constructive, but conviction is concentrated in a relatively narrow group of large-cap altcoins rather than across the full crypto market.
