Recent CryptoPulse reports consistently showed:
- BTC holding above US$80,000
- SOL repeatedly outperforming on 24h and 7d timeframes
- XRP strengthening on regulatory optimism
- ETH lagging higher-beta majors
- Market capitalisation stabilising near US$2.7tn
- Sentiment improving from neutral towards mildly risk-on

### What is changing

BTC dominance has remained elevated near 60%, which weakens the argument for a broad altcoin season despite strength in selected names. ETH still lacks convincing leadership characteristics, while XRP participation has strengthened further over the past several reports.

### What would confirm the trend

The trend would strengthen if:
- BTC remains above US$80k
- SOL and XRP continue outperforming over multiple reports
- BTC dominance stabilises or declines modestly
- Market capitalisation holds above US$2.7tn
- ETH begins participating more meaningfully

### What would invalidate the trend

The interpretation would weaken if:
- BTC loses US$80k support
- SOL underperforms sharply
- XRP loses regulatory-driven momentum
- BTC dominance rises materially further
- Macro or regulatory headlines trigger broader de-risking

### Analyst read

Fact: BTC remains stable while SOL and XRP continue outperforming across multiple timeframes. Interpretation: traders appear comfortable increasing exposure selectively, but conviction remains concentrated in a small group of higher-beta large caps rather than the broader market.
