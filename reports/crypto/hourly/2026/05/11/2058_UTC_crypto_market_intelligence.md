### Emerging trend

The dominant emerging pattern remains BTC-led consolidation combined with selective rotation into stronger large-cap altcoins including SOL, XRP, ADA and BNB.

### Evidence from recent reports

Recent CryptoPulse reports repeatedly identified:
- BTC stabilising above US$80,000
- SOL outperforming across multiple timeframes
- XRP maintaining momentum during regulatory-clarity discussions
- ETH lagging stronger altcoins
- Market capitalisation remaining near US$2.7tn
- Sentiment gradually improving from neutral towards mildly risk-on

### What is changing

Breadth improved modestly during the latest reports, with ADA participating more consistently alongside SOL and XRP. BTC dominance softened gradually over the latest several hours but remained elevated overall, limiting evidence of a broad altcoin regime. ETH improved incrementally but still lacked convincing leadership characteristics.

### What would confirm the trend

The trend would strengthen if:
- BTC breaks and holds above US$82k
- SOL and XRP continue outperforming across several more reports
- BTC dominance stabilises or declines further
- ETH begins outperforming BTC on shorter timeframes
- Market capitalisation expands beyond recent ranges

### What would invalidate the trend

The interpretation would weaken if:
- BTC loses US$80k support
- SOL momentum reverses sharply
- XRP loses relative strength
- BTC dominance rises materially further
- Macro or regulatory developments trigger broader de-risking
