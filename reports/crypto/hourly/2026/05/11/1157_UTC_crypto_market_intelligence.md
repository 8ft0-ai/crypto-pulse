## Rolling trend analysis

### Emerging trend

The strongest emerging pattern remains selective altcoin rotation within a BTC-led consolidation regime.

### Evidence from recent reports

Recent CryptoPulse reports consistently showed:
- BTC dominance holding near 58%
- BTC stabilising above US$80,000
- SOL repeatedly appearing among top performers
- XRP strengthening during regulatory-clarity discussions
- ETH remaining constructive but not dominant
- Stable overall market capitalisation near US$2.75tn–US$2.76tn

### What is changing

SOL leadership has become more persistent rather than episodic. XRP participation has broadened modestly, while ETH still lacks decisive leadership characteristics. BTC continues to act more as a stabilising anchor than a momentum driver.

### What would confirm the trend

The trend would strengthen if:
- BTC remains above US$80,000
- SOL continues outperforming on both 1h and 24h timeframes
- XRP maintains positive relative momentum
- BTC dominance eases without a fall in total market capitalisation

### What would invalidate the trend

The interpretation would weaken if:
- BTC loses the US$80,000 support region
- SOL sharply underperforms over several reports
- Breadth narrows back exclusively to BTC
- Macro or regulatory headlines trigger broader de-risking

### Analyst read

Fact: BTC remains stable above US$80,000 while SOL and XRP continue to outperform. Interpretation: market participants appear willing to selectively increase crypto risk exposure, but conviction remains concentrated rather than broad-based. Current conditions resemble controlled rotation rather than a speculative expansion phase.
