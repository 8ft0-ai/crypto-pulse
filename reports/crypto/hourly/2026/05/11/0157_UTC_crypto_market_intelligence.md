## Rolling trend analysis

### Emerging trend

The strongest emerging pattern is a BTC-led consolidation with improving selective altcoin rotation, rather than a broad, high-conviction market-wide breakout.

### Evidence from recent reports

- BTC has remained above the US$81,000 area across the latest accessible reports.
- BTC dominance has stayed near 58.3%, while ETH dominance has remained near 10.1%.
- ETH has participated in the recovery, but has not decisively led the market on the 7-day window.
- SOL, ADA, LINK, SUI, ZEC, TON and ONDO continue to appear as relative outperformers on multi-day measures.
- Sentiment has improved from fear toward neutral, but not into clear greed.
- ETF flows have become a recurring cautionary factor after a strong inflow streak reversed into outflows.

### What is changing

Market breadth has improved beyond BTC, especially in selected layer-1, DeFi, privacy and higher-beta tokens. However, the latest hour shows BTC and SOL pausing, which weakens the case for an immediate broad acceleration. ETH’s modest 1h relative strength is constructive, but its 7d performance remains weaker than SOL, ADA, SUI, ONDO and ZEC.

### What would confirm the trend

Confirmation would require BTC to hold above US$81,000 while ETH starts outperforming BTC over both 1h and 24h windows, and altcoin breadth remains positive without a liquidation spike. A return to positive ETF flows would strengthen the constructive interpretation.

### What would invalidate the trend

A BTC move back below the US$80,000 area, a reversal in SUI/ONDO/SOL/ADA strength, widening ETF outflows, or a confirmed liquidation surge would weaken the current selective risk-on interpretation.

### Analyst read

Fact: BTC remains above US$81,000, dominance is steady, and several liquid altcoins are outperforming over 24h and 7d windows. Interpretation: The market is not risk-off, but it is also not yet in a broad altcoin expansion; the most accurate description is selective altcoin rotation within a BTC-led recovery structure.
