## Rolling trend analysis

### Emerging trend

The strongest emerging pattern is controlled BTC consolidation above US$80,000 with selective large-cap altcoin rotation, led by SOL and XRP.

### Evidence from recent reports

Recent CryptoPulse baselines have repeatedly shown BTC dominance near 58%, ETH dominance near 10%, BTC holding around US$80,000, and stronger relative performance from selected large-cap altcoins rather than a full-market altcoin rally. The latest CoinGecko snapshot continues to show market capitalisation near US$2.75 trillion, BTC dominance near 58.3%, and SOL outperforming BTC and ETH on 1h, 24h and 7d measures. citeturn120470search0

### What is changing

SOL and XRP remain the clearest relative outperformers, while ETH’s positive hourly move has not translated into a stronger multi-day leadership signal. BTC is still structurally important but is acting more as a stabilising anchor than the main source of short-term momentum.

### What would confirm the trend

The trend would be confirmed if BTC continues to hold above US$80,000, SOL and XRP remain among the strongest large caps across the next few reports, and BTC dominance begins to soften without a fall in total crypto market capitalisation.

### What would invalidate the trend

The interpretation would weaken if BTC loses US$80,000, SOL gives back its 24-hour and 7-day strength, XRP loses its regulatory-news bid, or market breadth narrows back to BTC alone.

### Analyst read

Fact: BTC remains near US$80,000, total market capitalisation is broadly stable near US$2.75 trillion, and SOL and XRP are outperforming in the latest available market and news data. Interpretation: the market is in a selective-risk regime, not a broad altcoin expansion. Regulatory optimism is helping parts of the altcoin complex, but incomplete liquidation and ETF-flow data keep confidence below high.
