## Rolling trend analysis

### Emerging trend

The clearest emerging pattern remains BTC-led consolidation combined with selective rotation into stronger large-cap altcoins including SOL, XRP, ADA and BNB.

### Evidence from recent reports

Recent reports repeatedly identified BTC stabilising above US$80,000, SOL outperforming across multiple timeframes, XRP maintaining momentum during regulatory-clarity discussions, ETH lagging stronger altcoins, market capitalisation remaining near US$2.7tn, and sentiment gradually improving from neutral towards mildly risk-on.

### What is changing

Breadth improved modestly, with ADA participating more consistently alongside SOL and XRP. BTC dominance remained elevated near 60%, limiting evidence of a broad altcoin regime. ETH improved slightly but still lacked convincing leadership characteristics.

### What would confirm the trend

Further confirmation would require BTC holding above US$80k, SOL and XRP continuing to outperform, BTC dominance stabilising or declining, ETH beginning to outperform BTC on shorter timeframes, and market capitalisation expanding above recent ranges.

### What would invalidate the trend

A BTC loss of US$80k support, sharp SOL reversal, XRP loss of relative strength, materially higher BTC dominance, or broader macro/regulatory de-risking would weaken the interpretation.

### Analyst read

Fact: BTC remains stable while SOL, XRP and ADA continue outperforming across multiple reporting windows. Interpretation: market participants appear selectively comfortable adding crypto exposure, but conviction remains concentrated rather than broad-based.
