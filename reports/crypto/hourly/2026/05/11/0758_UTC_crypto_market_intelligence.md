## Rolling trend analysis

### Emerging trend

BTC-led consolidation with selective altcoin rotation remains the clearest emerging pattern.

### Evidence from recent reports

Recent available market snapshots consistently show:
- BTC dominance remaining elevated around the high-50% region.
- ETH stabilising and modestly outperforming BTC on shorter timeframes.
- ADA, LINK and SOL repeatedly appearing among relative outperformers.
- Stablecoin pegs remaining stable.
- Institutional and regulatory narratives continuing to influence sentiment. citeturn0search0turn0news28turn0news26

### What is changing

ETH strength has improved modestly relative to BTC, and broader large-cap breadth appears healthier than during earlier defensive phases. However, BTC dominance remains high enough that this still looks more like selective rotation than a full alt-season transition.

### What would confirm the trend

Confirmation would require:
- BTC maintaining the US$80,000+ region across several more reports.
- ETH dominance beginning to rise materially.
- Broader participation from large-cap altcoins with sustained volume growth.
- Reduced dependence on BTC-only flows.

### What would invalidate the trend

The interpretation would weaken if:
- BTC loses the US$80,000 area decisively.
- ETH resumes underperformance.
- Market breadth narrows sharply.
- Macro or regulatory headlines trigger broad risk-off selling.

### Analyst read

Fact: BTC dominance remains elevated while ETH and several liquid altcoins are outperforming on shorter timeframes. Interpretation: the market appears cautiously constructive, but conditions still resemble controlled consolidation rather than a confirmed broad speculative expansion.
