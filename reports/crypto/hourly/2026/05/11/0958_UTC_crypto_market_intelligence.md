## Rolling trend analysis

### Emerging trend

The strongest emerging pattern is controlled BTC consolidation above US$80,000 with selective altcoin rotation, led most clearly by SOL and XRP in the current hour.

### Evidence from recent reports

Recent CryptoPulse baselines have repeatedly shown BTC dominance elevated near 58%, ETH dominance near 10%, BTC holding the US$80,000 area, and selective strength across liquid large-cap altcoins rather than a uniform market-wide rally. The current market snapshot continues that structure, with total crypto market capitalisation near US$2.75 trillion and BTC dominance at about 58.3%. citeturn681501search0

### What is changing

SOL has strengthened materially versus the previous report’s more modest positive reading, while XRP has improved from lagging to outperforming among the large caps. ETH remains positive but its leadership has weakened compared with earlier reports where ETH was one of the clearest outperformers.

### What would confirm the trend

The trend would be confirmed if BTC continues to hold above US$80,000, SOL and XRP remain among the leading large caps across multiple reports, and BTC dominance begins to ease without a deterioration in total market capitalisation.

### What would invalidate the trend

The interpretation would weaken if BTC loses US$80,000, SOL gives back its 24-hour strength, XRP fails to hold its regulatory-news bid, or market breadth narrows back to BTC alone.

### Analyst read

Fact: BTC remains near the US$80,000–US$81,000 area, total market capitalisation is broadly stable near US$2.75 trillion, and SOL and XRP are currently stronger than BTC on short-term measures. Interpretation: the market is not yet in a broad altcoin expansion, but risk appetite is improving selectively where assets have clearer catalysts or stronger recent momentum.
