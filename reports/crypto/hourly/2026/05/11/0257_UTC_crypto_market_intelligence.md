## Rolling trend analysis

### Emerging trend

The strongest emerging pattern remains a BTC-led consolidation accompanied by selective high-beta altcoin rotation rather than a broad speculative surge.

### Evidence from recent reports

- BTC has consistently held above the US$81,000 region.
- ETH has slightly improved relative performance, but still trails several leading altcoins on the 7-day timeframe.
- SOL, ADA, SUI, ONDO, ZEC and now XMR repeatedly appear among relative outperformers.
- BTC dominance remains elevated above 58%, although it has softened slightly over the latest hours.
- Sentiment has stabilised in neutral territory rather than moving decisively into greed.

### What is changing

Altcoin participation is broadening modestly beyond layer-1 tokens into DeFi and privacy assets. ETH dominance has ticked slightly higher, but not enough to suggest an ETH-led regime shift. BTC itself appears to be consolidating rather than accelerating.

### What would confirm the trend

Further confirmation would require BTC to remain stable above US$81,000 while ETH dominance and altcoin breadth continue improving without a major leverage unwind. Sustained gains in ADA, SUI, ONDO and privacy tokens over multiple reports would reinforce the selective-rotation interpretation.

### What would invalidate the trend

A sharp BTC rejection below US$80,000, renewed dominance expansion without breadth participation, or a sudden liquidation cascade would weaken the constructive interpretation. Persistent ETF outflows would also increase downside risk.

### Analyst read

Fact: BTC dominance remains high, but selective altcoins continue outperforming across multiple reports. Interpretation: The market structure currently resembles cautious risk-on rotation rather than a euphoric alt-season or defensive consolidation.
